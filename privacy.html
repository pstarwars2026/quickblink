Privacy, plainly.

The app

QuickBlink does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your results, badges and settings are stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them at any time in Settings → Reset All Results.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional and use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best round median for a mode is sent to Apple and may appear on public leaderboards with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Sharing

Sharing a score card only happens when you tap Share, and goes wherever you choose to send it.

This website

This site adds no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions

Open a support issue. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information.
